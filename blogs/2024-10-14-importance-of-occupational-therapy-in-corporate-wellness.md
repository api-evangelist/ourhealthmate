In today’s fast-paced corporate environment, employee wellness is no longer a luxury—it’s a necessity. Companies worldwide are increasingly recognizing the importance of prioritizing employee well-being to boost productivity, reduce turnover, and create a healthier workplace culture. One often overlooked but incredibly impactful tool in corporate wellness programs is Occupational Therapy (OT).
